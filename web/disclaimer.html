    About the rules content

    The rules summaries in the app (spells, feats, classes, species, backgrounds, conditions and equipment) are short paraphrases written for quick reference at the table. They are not the full rules.

    Some of this material is covered by the System Reference Document 5.2 (SRD 5.2), which Wizards of the Coast releases under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Some of it isn't. For example, material first published in Xanathar's Guide to Everything, Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse and Eberron: Rising from the Last War. That material remains the property of Wizards of the Coast and is included only as non-commercial fan content. The project claims no rights to it.

    If you represent Wizards of the Coast and would like something changed or removed, please open an issue on GitHub and it will be taken down.

    For the full rules, use the official books or the System Reference Document. If you play D&D, please support it by buying the official rulebooks.
