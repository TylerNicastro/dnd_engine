    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 17, 2026

    D&D Engine is a free fan project that runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers run by this project, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies.

    What is stored, and where

    • Your characters, homebrew, tags and content filters are saved by the browser version in your browser's IndexedDB storage, on your device only. They are never uploaded to us. The desktop version saves them to files in its own folder on your computer.
    • Two small preferences are kept in your browser's localStorage: whether you dismissed the storage notice, and whether the app should launch automatically.
    • Backups you export with Backup & Restore are saved where your browser or computer puts downloaded files. Files you import are read on your device and are not sent anywhere.
    • The site search in the header runs entirely in your browser and doesn't send what you type anywhere.

    Because everything stays on your device, clearing your site data, using a private window, or switching browsers or devices means your saves won't be there. Use Backup & Restore → Export JSON on the app's main menu to keep a copy you control.

    Third-party services

    • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the desktop download. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request information (such as your IP address and browser type) when you load a page or download a file. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
    • CheerpJ, by Leaning Technologies, provides the in-browser Java runtime. It's downloaded from their servers (cjrtnc.leaningtech.com) when you launch the app, and those servers receive standard request information. See leaningtech.com for their policies.

    The About, Download and legal pages don't load CheerpJ; only the Main App page does.

    Your choices

    You can delete everything this site has stored at any time by clearing site data for this website in your browser settings. Export a backup first if you want to keep your characters. For the desktop version, delete characters.json and custom_assets.json from the app's folder.

    Children

    This site is not directed at children under 13. We don't collect, store or process personal information from anyone, including children, because all character data stays on your own device.

    Contact

    Questions or concerns? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
